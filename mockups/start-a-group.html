Start a group

A group is a room in the commons. Invite people you already know to reveal hidden opportunities and resources - work, shared tools, new ways to collaborate. You decide who's welcome, how you show up together, and what you'd like to see happen.

What is this group called?*

Short and specific reads best. "Seattle Tech Network" or "Snohomish Green Build" both land clearly.

In a sentence, what is this group for?*

Say who it's for and what they'll find here. This is the line people read before they decide to join.

Add a cover image

One image that represents your group. Paste a link for now — upload comes later.

🖼️
Group passcode*

Every group has a passcode. It's how someone joins your group when they land on Creating.Works — they add your passcode and jump straight in. We suggest one based on your group's name; you can change it.
