Top bar, three ways

Same tab map in all three, drawn three different ways. Nothing on this page navigates. The question each one answers is where the bar stops and the page begins, and whether Appear's tabs live on our bar or stay on Appear's.

Design A · The 2Gather bar

One bar per product. Groups and Gather are ours, so our pages carry the 2Gather bar, the same one the 2Gather app already shows. Appear keeps its own bar for Network, Opportunities, your page and invites. Crossing between them is a deliberate step, not a blur.

Design B · One bar across both

Every place a person can go, on one strip, in the order they think about them. The tabs we have migrated open our pages. The tabs still in Appear open Appear. As each area moves across, a tab quietly changes where it points and nothing else changes.

What it does to the pills. Same as A, they are absorbed into the bar. Cost. The bar says Appear while the page underneath is ours, so the migration stays invisible to people, and the 2Gather name never gets a home. It also means our pages have to keep drawing Appear's tabs correctly forever.

Design C · The 2Gather bar with a context line

Design A on top, plus a thin line underneath carrying where you are and what you can do here. This is the one that answers the group page, which today has a breadcrumb and a My profile pill of its own. The bar says where you can go, the line says where you are.
