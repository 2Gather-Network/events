Marks for showing up

The mark is the 2Gather mark itself. It starts as a single flare and grows three times, and the last step is the logo, whole. Somebody who keeps showing up is not collecting a score, they are assembling the thing they are part of.

The ladder

Four steps. The flare alone, the flare larger, the flare taking its tile, then the full mark at its proper size.

Why the flare first. One shape, one colour, no outline, so it survives at 16px where a ring or a cluster of circles turns to grey mush. Why the logo last. The final step is not a bigger version of a decoration, it is the mark of the thing itself, which is the only step that needs to feel like arriving.

The four lines open on the page under the count, never in an overlay. On Attending and Hosting the same line reads "2 coming up" and "17 coming up".

The rules this has to keep

  • Nobody reads the words badge, level or tier. The line thanks them for what they did. In the code and the sheet, badge and badgeTier stay as names.
  • Simple past, so "You brought people to 115 gatherings", never "you have brought".
  • The tab counts are events coming up and they fall as events pass. The mark counts lifetime totals, attended, hosted and socialized, so a mark can never take itself away.
  • No extra call. My events already builds the three lists in memory, and the totals are their lengths.

The one open question

  • At 115 socialized you hold the top step and nobody is near it, which makes the last mark a private trophy rather than an invitation. Bringing the steps down to 3, 10, 25 and 50 keeps four real steps, puts the first inside somebody's first month, and leaves the top rare without making it yours alone.
