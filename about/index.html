About Let's Gather

People-led gatherings in person and online, where what somebody brings circulates back into the community around them.

Here is how to get started.

👥 Attend an event

Find events with people who share your interests. Filter by category, by format, whether that is virtual, in person or both, and by location, host or date range.

See what is happening

🪪 Post an event

Post your event and the people already looking for that kind of gathering will see it. Every event links to a 2Gather room, or to your own meeting link if you would rather use one. Add an event by signing in and filling out the form, or by pasting the text you already wrote about it.
