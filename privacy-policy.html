Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026Previous version

Our privacy principles

  1. Your data belongs to you rather than to us.
  2. We do not sell or trade your information.
  3. We built our business to serve you and you only.

We honor each individual guest's agency, data sovereignty, identity, and privacy. This is above and beyond all else, no exceptions. This is an absolute.

Creating Works welcomes you the way a good host welcomes a guest, with care, with room to be yourself and with nothing hidden. You sign in once and carry one profile across all of our tools, including 2Appear, 2Gather, and 2Balance. What you share in one place affects what reaches you in the others, and this policy covers all of them together.

Creating Works is operated by DayBalancer LLC. We treat your data with the same care and respect we would our own. We run a platform where you define, express, and share who you are, what you offer and what you seek. There are no hidden revenue streams and no data-sharing arrangements with advertisers.

We designed our business model around protecting your information. We hold transparent and ethical relationships with the people who use our platform.

This policy describes the software as it runs today. Where a protection is planned rather than built, we say so in the section that covers it. We would rather tell you what is here now than describe something you cannot yet rely on.

This policy sits alongside our terms of service and our code of conduct.

1Information we collect

We collect the following information so you can share what you offer and find what you seek:

  • Personal information: your name / email addresses / phone number / photo / location / time zone.
  • What you offer: your skills / experience / interests / values.
  • What you seek: the needs you choose to post, along with any detail you write about them. Some of these categories touch on health and money and you decide which ones to fill in.
  • Personal preferences you choose to share and use for connections: information about your work style / work preferences / communication patterns / stress factors / learning preferences. This helps us understand which resources you want shared with you.
  • Connection preferences you choose to share and use for connections: your objectives / availability / desired compensation / the factors you want considered when you want a suggested connection.
  • Event information you choose to share and use for connections: the events you host / register for / what you bring to them.
  • Technical data: the companies that host our pages keep their own server logs and those logs can hold your IP address and browser type.

2How we use your information

  • Connect you with people and opportunities: when you say you want matches, we match you with people and opportunities that reflect what you care about, what you offer, and what you need.
  • Provide compatibility analysis: when you say you want compatible matches, we generate compatibility assessments so both you and others can make an informed decision.
  • Support your work: we help you see your strengths so that you can grow the work you want.
  • Improve our services: we look at how people use our tools so the platform works better for the next person.
  • Communicate with you: we answer your questions and send updates about our services. You opt in to those updates when you create your profile and you can update and manage notifications for all emails you receive.

3Where your information sits and who can see it

One profile carries you across all three tools, in addition to Creating Works. This section describes how that works today and we want you to read it before you fill in a profile.

Behind a sign-in: With the exception of your public profile page you enable for people to see, everything opens only after you sign in with a six-digit code sent to your email or with Google. The events calendar, public events, and public profile pages are open to anyone.

Open by link: Any profile pages on 2Appear or 2Gather are seen by anyone holding the link that you agreed to share. You choose what goes on your 2Appear page and 2Gather event profile. You can take anything off it at any time. Before you type something into a need, consider whether you would say it to a stranger. This covers everything on the page, including the needs you post and the detail you write about them. We tell search engines not to list these pages, but anyone who has the link can still open them.

When you or someone is seen as a match, you each decide to stay anonymous or be seen.

4How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data in these limited circumstances:

  • People you share with: anyone holding a link to your page, as section 3 describes.
  • Service providers: the companies listed in section 5, who hold and serve the data so the platform runs.
  • Legal requirements: we disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights or prevent harm to someone.

5Companies that receive your information

These are the companies our software relies on:

  • Google: holds the data behind your profile in Google and runs code that reads and saves it.
  • Railway: hosts answers you give. Railway is a hosting company based in the United States.
  • Cloudflare: sits in front of our web addresses and passes requests. It carries your sign-in from one of our sites to another, stores the messages you send and the photos you upload, and runs our short-link and AI services that you opt into.
  • Zoho: sends the emails we write to you.
  • GitHub: serves the pages of our sites.
  • Squarespace: serves our DayBalancer website.

Each of these companies holds your information under its own privacy terms and we choose them for what they let us build rather than for what they let us collect.

6Artificial intelligence

We build parts of our software with the help of AI assistants. Your personal information is not put into an AI model for training. If we build a feature where AI reads what you wrote, we ask you before it touches your information, we tell you how it works and what it learns, and you can hold that consent back on its own without giving up anything else.

7Security

  • Connections: every page loads over HTTPS, so what travels between your browser and our pages is encrypted along the way.
  • Storage: your answers rest in two places, Google and Railway controls our database information. Messaging and photos rest with Cloudflare. Google and Cloudflare encrypt stored data on their own infrastructure.
  • Access: you reach your account by signing in with your email address. The pages you share by link carry no password, so the link itself is what opens them. Inside our company, the people who run the platform can open profile data records. Direct messages are only shown to the two people in the conversation. Our product has no screen or tool that lets staff read them. They are not encrypted by us, so someone with technical access to our database could open them.
  • Review: we read this policy against the running software whenever we change either one. The last read was November 20, 2026.

8What we store in your browser

The pages we write run no advertising scripts.

We use two cookies of our own. One remembers the state you chose when you look for events, for a year. The other keeps you signed in across our sites for as long as your sign-in lasts. It holds only a random code, never your name or email. Neither is used for advertising. Google sign-in and Google fonts may set their own.

Your browser also keeps your member ID, your sign-in key (which expires), and your first name and photo, so you stay signed in and pages remember you between visits. When you move between our sites, we pass your sign-in with a one-time code, and the secure cookie keeps you signed in on the others. Signing out or clearing your browser storage removes them. None of it goes to advertisers.

9Your data rights

You hold the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal information.
  • Correction: request an update to information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Deletion: request removal of your information from our systems.
  • Restriction: limit how we process certain information.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured / machine-readable format.
  • Objection: contest a particular processing activity.

To exercise any of these, write to hello@creating.works. A person reads that mailbox and carries out the request by hand and we answer within 30 days. There is no self-serve export or deletion button on the site yet. You can also edit or remove most of what appears on your own page yourself at any time.

We keep your information for as long as you have a profile with us. When you ask us to delete it, a person removes it within 30 days.

10Regional privacy regulations

We honor the rights that the General Data Protection Regulation grants people in Europe and the rights that the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act grant California residents. That covers the right to know what we hold / the right to correction / the right to deletion / the right to portability / the right to opt out of any sale of personal information. We sell no personal information, so the last of those has nothing to act on.

Every one of these requests is handled by a person at hello@creating.works rather than by an automated tool. When your data crosses a border it does so inside the infrastructure of the companies named in section 5, under the transfer terms each of them publishes.

11Age 18 and older

Our services are for people who are 18 or older. During registration we ask you to confirm that you are at least 18 and we record your answer with the date you gave it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we hold information from someone under 18, we delete it promptly. If you believe we hold information about a person under 18, write to hello@creating.works.

12What happens to your information over time

Our operating agreement holds no shares that can be sold and no equity that appreciates, so there is no exit event that could carry your information off as part of a new agreement. Moving substantially all of what the company holds takes the consent of every member and the agreement names data stewardship as an obligation each member carries personally.

This agreement sends what remains to the commons or to a community organization that shares our purpose. Your personal information moves only to an organization that holds it under this policy. We tell you before that happens and you can export or delete your information first.

13Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change or when the law calls for it. We tell you about a significant change by updating the effective date at the foot of this page and by sending notice through email or through the platform. This version takes effect on November 20, 2026 and replaces the version of October 22, 2026. Earlier versions stay published at their own addresses, linked from the foot of this page.
