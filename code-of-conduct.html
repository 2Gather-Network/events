Code of conduct (CoC)

Effective November 9, 2026Previous version

CoC means code of conduct. Where we meet other people on 2Gather and 2Appear, we build together and serve the common good. This is how we treat each other here.

2Balance is just for you, so this CoC does not apply for 2Balance.

This CoC sits alongside our terms of service and our privacy policy.

Respect

Collaborate, don't promote. Here we work together on the challenges we share. Save promotion for the people who have already shown interest in what you offer. Instead of promoting your skills or solution, ask: "How could we address [this problem] together?" or "How might this idea help all of us?"

  • Respect for all people: treat all members, guests, and hosts with respect and courtesy. Recognize the time, skills, and views that each person contributes.
  • Inclusive behavior: do not take part in discrimination, harassment, gossip, or bullying based on race, color, gender identity/expression, national origin, age, religion, marital status, disability, neurodiversity, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.
  • Kind and clear words: keep your messages, profile and feedback respectful and clear.
  • Constructive engagement: provide thoughtful, constructive feedback when requested. Focus on what your interactions give the other person.
  • Accurate representation: describe your skills, experience, what you care about, and values as they are.
  • Keep learning: take part in what happens here and think about the feedback others give you.

Confidentiality and privacy

  • Confidentiality: respect what people share in confidence. Do not pass on sensitive or business information unless they say you can.
  • Privacy settings: honor each person's privacy settings and do not try to get around the protections that keep them private.
  • Photos, names and sharing: do not copy, share, or post anyone's photo, name, or profile outside our sites without their yes. At events, show people the way they chose to be shown.
  • Group analytics: all members of a group can see the group's analytics. They are shared only with the members of that group. They are counts only, with no names, including information such as how many jobs were filled and how many introductions were made in a group.
  • Groups, chats and events: what you see on groups, chats, or events stays with the people there. Do not share it elsewhere without their consent.

Ethical use of our sites

  • Appropriate requests: use 2Gather and 2Appear to make connections that serve what you and others care about.
  • Respect for boundaries: honor the preferences and boundaries set by others, including their availability and how they like to be contacted.
  • Feedback integrity: give clear, constructive feedback about connections and experiences so everyone's experience keeps getting better.

Compliance

  • Legal compliance: abide by all applicable local, state, and federal laws when using our sites and meeting other people.
  • Our policies: follow our terms of service and privacy policy. Breaking this CoC, the terms or the policy may limit or end what you can do here.

Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks this CoC, tell us through Support: choose "Report conduct concern" from the drop-down, or email hello@creating.works. Say what happened, who was involved, when, and anything that shows it.

You can also manage email notifications for all sites.

Telling us you have read it

Here it is. We would love you to read it. When you have finished reading, we would like you to tell us you have read it.

Together we make a kind, useful community built on meaningful connections.
