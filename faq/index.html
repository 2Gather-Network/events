Getting started

Any page

How do I put 2Gather on my phone's home screen?

It sits beside your other apps, and one press opens it.

iPhone, in Safari

  1. Open 2gather.network in Safari.
  2. Press the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Press Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll down to see it.
  4. Press Add.

iPhone, in Brave or Chrome

  1. Open 2gather.network.
  2. Press the Share button. In Brave it is in the ••• menu at the bottom right.
  3. Press Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android, in Chrome

  1. Open 2gather.network in Chrome.
  2. Press the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Press Add to Home screen or Install app, then Add.
What is 2Gather?

2Gather is where people in a community find things to do together, post their own events, and gather in groups.

Do I need an account?

No, not to look. Anybody can browse the calendar and open a public event.

To post an event, bookmark one, register, socialize it or be part of a group, press Sign in at the top right.

How do I get help or report a problem?

Press Support at the top of any page, choose what it is about, and write to us.

Finding events

Calendar

How do I find an event?

Open the Calendar and type in Search events, or narrow it with the filters: Location, Date, Categories, Groups, Format, Experience, Venue and Host.

On a phone the filters are folded under the Filters button. It says how many are on.

Where do I find an event within my group?

On the Calendar, Group events lists the events of the groups you are in. To see one group's events only, open Groups in the filters and choose it.

You can also press My groups at the top and open the group itself.

What are Recommended matches?

Events that fit your interests, within the filters you have on, such as location and format. The round i beside the heading says the same.

How do I change my interests?

Open Interests at the top of the filters. Type a topic and press Add, or pick from the things your profile says you enjoy.

How do I see only the events I bookmarked?

Turn on Show bookmarked at the bottom of the filters. It appears once you have bookmarked something.

Why don't I see cancelled events?

They are hidden so the calendar shows what is happening. Turn on Show cancelled to see them.

How do I clear my filters?

Press Reset at the bottom of the filters.

An event's page

Any event, opened from the Calendar

How do I save an event for later?

Press Bookmark beside the event's title. It turns blue and reads Bookmarked. Press it again to take it off.

You will not see Bookmark on events you host, because those are already under Hosting on My events.

How do I add an event to my own calendar?

Press Add to calendar on the event's page.

How do I register or pay?

When the host asks people to register, a Register / Pay button sits beside the title. When the host has registration off, there is nothing to sign up for. Just come.

What does Socialize this event do?

It helps you spread the word. It opens a screen with Copy link, Share and a QR code, and asks whether you would like to be listed as helping.

If you say yes, your name shows under Socialized by on the event, and the event is kept for you under Socialized on My events.

How do I share an event with a friend?

Press Socialize this event, then Copy link or Share. Showing somebody the QR code works too.

What do Hosted by and Socialized by mean?

Hosted by is the person running the event, and the organization they represent if there is one. Socialized by lists the people helping spread the word who chose to be shown.

I host an event somebody else posted. How do I take it over?

Press Socialize this event and then Are you the host of this event?. It opens the Support page with Request to claim event as host chosen and the event named. Once it is approved, you can edit it.

Posting an event

Post an event

How do I post an event?

Press Post a new event on My events, or the + beside Search events on the Calendar.

Choose I'm hosting or I'm sharing. Then either Fill out form, or Share event details by pasting a link or some text, or uploading a picture, and the form is filled for you. Check it and press Post event.

What is the difference between I'm hosting and I'm sharing?

I'm hosting means it is your event. It is kept under Hosting, and you can ask people to register.

I'm sharing means somebody else runs it and you are spreading the word. It is kept under Socialized, and registration stays with the real host.

Can I post from a flyer or a web page?

Yes. Choose Share event details, upload a picture of the flyer or paste the page's link or text, and press Parse and fill form. Read the form over before you post, because a date or time can be misread.

How do I make an event repeat?

Tick Repeating event in the form, choose how often, and whether it lands on the same weekday or the same date each time.

How do I make an event private?

Under Visibility, choose Private. A private event is not on the public calendar, so send its link to the people you are inviting.

How do I connect an event to my group?

The form lists the groups you are in. Tick the group it belongs to. Posting from the group's own page ticks it for you.

Does registration have to be on?

No. Event registration starts Off. Choose Optional or Required if you want people to sign up.

How do I add a picture?

Under Cover image, press Upload, or paste a link to the picture itself. A link to the web page the picture is on will not show a picture.

My events

My events

What are Attending, Hosting and Socialized?

Attending is what you registered for. Hosting is what you host or co-host. Socialized is what you shared or are helping spread the word about.

Where is the event I just posted?

Under Hosting if you chose I'm hosting, or Socialized if you chose I'm sharing.

How do I change or cancel my event?

On My events, press Hosting and then the event, and its form opens. Change what you need and save. To call it off, use Mark as cancelled.

On the event's own page, hosts can also press Edit.

How do I find an event again quickly?

Type part of its name in Search on My events, or change the order with Soonest first, Furthest out or A to Z.

On my phone, where did the rest of my events go?

Once an event is open, the others fold behind the Events bar so the event fills the screen. Press the bar to see them all again.

Groups

My groups

Where do I see my groups?

Press My groups at the top of the Calendar or My events. It shows once you are in a group.

How do I join a group?

Open the group's page from the link its host shares. Every group has a passcode, which the group's host gives you.

How do I post an event for my group?

Press Post an event on the group's page. The group is ticked for you in the form.
