What fits this group?

Members choose what each group sees of them. This sets what is best suited for your group to ensure there is a good fit.

Pick what your group is really for. People carry all sorts of things in their profile, and this keeps your group to the kinds that belong here. You can change it whenever you like.

Paid or not. A job, an internship, volunteering, mentoring, or a project somebody can join.
