Permanent changes

Each of these re-checks your admin PIN before it runs. Transferring the group and archiving it cannot be undone.

Reset passcode
Sends a new passcode to your email. Anyone with the old one can no longer join.
Transfer host
Offer this group to another member. Nothing changes until they accept. You stay a member.
Archive group
The group stops being somewhere anybody can join or gather. Its people keep their own profiles and their history.

Archiving needs the code we email you. It expires in 15 minutes and can only be used once.
