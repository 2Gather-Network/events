The plus on + Other

Every other chip opens with an emoji and + Other opens with a typed plus, so it sits lighter than its neighbours and reads as an afterthought. Three weights to choose from. The first is what is there now. The second is the same typed plus, made heavier and set at the size the emoji occupies, which keeps it a character the browser draws in your own colour. The third is the heavy plus emoji itself, which matches the others literally and is drawn by the phone, so it carries its own colour and will not match your blue.
