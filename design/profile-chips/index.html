Design B — the panel carries it, so the chips stop shouting

Same panel, but once the boundary says these are on your profile the chips no longer need to. They go white with a thin border, the level turns grey, and the panel is the only filled thing on the screen. Seven of them read as a list of what you have rather than seven buttons. This is the one I would build: it fixes the wall of blue that made the first screen hard to look at, and the meaning stops depending on a colour, which matters for anyone who cannot tell your blue from your grey.
