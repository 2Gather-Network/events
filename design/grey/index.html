What this page is

The same pieces of the site drawn three times, differing only in the grey. That grey is on 133 lines across the site — help lines, category names under a card, levels under a chip, counts, placeholders. The everyday standard asks for 4.5 to 1 against what is behind the text; the stricter one asks for 7. Today’s grey reaches 4.4 on white and 3.95 on the pale blue panels. The numbers under each heading are measured, not estimated. Nothing here changes the real site.
