What this page is

Two changes on the questions page, drawn together because they belong together. First, the Cares cards take the look of the offering cards you liked: a pale blue card with a thin border instead of a white one. Second, the collapsing bar that Cares already has goes onto Needs and Offerings as well. Put together, every step that collects things now uses one card and one bar, so a person learns the look once. Nothing saves and the real page is untouched. The level buttons and the collapse bars work, so try them.
