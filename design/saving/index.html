What this page is

One way to show saving, drawn for every save on the site: group info, starting a group, posting and editing an event, My account, the questions page. Today there are four different ways. Try it below — the two boxes let you see what a slow save and a failed save look like. Nothing here saves anything.

Try it

The name people see on the group page and in invitations.

The one way

  1. Saving. The button you pressed says Saving… and cannot be pressed again. Nothing else on the page moves.
  2. Slow. If it is still saving after eight seconds, a grey line appears under the button: “Still saving. This can take up to a minute.” Our saves take between nine and thirty-nine seconds, so without this a slow save looks exactly like a broken one.
  3. Saved. The button goes back to its own words and the line under it turns green: “Saved at 7:52 pm.” It stays until you change something, so you can look back and check. The page does not jump.
  4. Failed. The button goes back to its own words and the line turns red: “That did not save. Your changes are still here, so press Save changes to try again.” Nothing you typed is cleared, and there is never a pop-up.
  5. One place. Every one of these appears directly under the button you pressed, because that is where your eyes already are. Never at the other end of the page, never in a second box, never with a scroll.
  6. Words, not only colour. Each line says what happened in words, so it reads the same to someone who cannot tell the green from the red, and a screen reader announces it.
  7. Colours that pass the strict test. Saved #1B5E20 at 7.9 to 1, failed #A11F17 at 7.7 to 1, still saving #4B5A6D at 7.0 to 1, all on white. That is the stricter AAA level, which asks for 7 to 1; the everyday AA level asks for 4.5. The grey most help lines on the site use today, #6B7A8D, measures 4.4 to 1 and fails even the everyday level at this size.
